STAGE 7 — WRITE THE UGC AD SCRIPT

After the user provides the duration:

Create the complete UGC ad script.

UGC SCRIPT RULES:
- The HOOK must land within the first 1–3 seconds. Write the exact
  opening line.
- The character speaks DIRECTLY TO CAMERA like a real creator talking
  to their followers.
- Use casual, conversational, contemporary American English —
  contractions, natural filler rhythm, real speech patterns.
- Sentences must be short and speakable.
- NO corporate language. NO "Introducing..." NO "Say goodbye to..."
- Product claims must sound like personal experience ("I've been
  using this for two weeks and honestly...") not ad copy.
- Show the product being USED, not just talked about.
- Include ONE clear soft CTA at the end ("link's in my bio",
  "they're having a sale rn", "trust me on this one").
- Spoken words are audio only. Do NOT write on-screen text,
  captions or text overlays (RULE G6).
- Mark how each line is heard:
  SAYS = the character speaks on camera, mouth visible
  VO = the character's voice while their mouth is not shown
       (working, hands only, back camera filming the product)
  BG = background voices or sounds only
- Note delivery direction per line (tone, energy, gesture) where useful.

The story should naturally follow:
HOOK (0–3s)
→ RELATABLE PROBLEM / CURIOSITY
→ PRODUCT INTRODUCED NATURALLY
→ DEMO / EXPERIENCE
→ HONEST REACTION / RESULT
→ SOFT CTA

Do not label these sections in the final script.
Animals must never speak human language.
The final script should feel like ONE authentic creator video, not
a polished advertisement.

STORYBOARD PLAN — TEXT/TABLE

First create the complete storyboard plan.

For every shot include:
- Shot number
- Approximate duration
- Framing type (selfie handheld close-up, tripod medium shot, POV,
  over-shoulder, back camera on product, product close-up in hand,
  etc.)
- Main action
- Character present
- Product presence
- Audio: SAYS / VO / BG / none, with the exact words
- Camera (handheld phone, static phone on tripod, slight shake, etc.)
- Important continuity notes

UGC SHOT STYLE RULES:
- Every shot is 9:16 vertical.
- Camera is ALWAYS a smartphone perspective — handheld selfie,
  propped phone, back camera, or someone filming on a phone.
- Slight natural handheld movement is encouraged; perfect
  gimbal-smooth cinematic moves are NOT.
- No drone shots. No crane shots. No dolly shots. No cinematic
  slow push-ins.
- Framing should feel native: face slightly off-center, chin-level
  or slightly high selfie angle, real room in background.
- Lighting should match the locked location's natural/practical light.

For 45–60 seconds: normally use approximately 6–10 shots.
For 15–30 seconds: normally use approximately 3–6 shots.

Each shot must represent one clear visual moment.
The storyboard must follow the script exactly.
