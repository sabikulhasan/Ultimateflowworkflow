Commercial Ad Prompt Builder

Fast-cut commercials with flashy edits, sound effects and on-screen text. Fill in the product, pick a length and pace, check the shot list, then copy the prompts: ChatGPT draws the storyboards, Google Flow shoots the clips, and the cut sheet tells you exactly how to edit them in CapCut.

1Market and language

Sets the ready-made audiences and models, and the language of anything a person says on camera or in the voiceover.

2Product and brand

Type freely. Fields with ▾ also have ready-made options. Only the product name, category and selling point are needed; the rest makes the ad sharper.

Optional: reference sheets. A product sheet keeps the product the same across many fast cuts. Put your own photos in the template and download a PNG, then attach it in ChatGPT with the storyboard prompt and tag it in Flow. Skip this if a few good product photos are enough.

3Length, pace and style

Length and pace decide how many frames the ad needs and how long each one is. Flow can't make clips shorter than 4 seconds, so the tool plans the clips for you: short shots are shot inside longer clips, with spare seconds at both ends for trimming.

4Shot list

The format fills in every frame. Change anything. Each frame is one shot in the finished ad. The colored bar shows how the frames are grouped into Flow clips; a gold block is a text card that is made separately.

5On-screen text

Add text to any frame in its On-screen text box. Each text line picks one of three levels. Auto picks for you: short English text goes to Flow, and longer or Bangla text becomes an image card.

Text look
End card
Level 1 · Flow draws it. Up to 4 English words. The text and its animation are written into the storyboard and the Flow prompt. Cheapest, but check the spelling in every clip.
Level 2 · Image, then animate. ChatGPT makes the frame with the text (it spells well, Bangla too). Flow animates only the scene around the letters; CapCut does the entrance. Best for end cards and offers.
Level 3 · Add in CapCut. The clip stays clean. The cut sheet gives the text, time, position and the exact animation to set.

8Edit in CapCut with the cut sheet
