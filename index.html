UGC Ad Prompt Builder

Pick options, copy each ready-made message, and paste it into ChatGPT when it asks. For the US, the master prompt is copied word for word; the Bangladesh version changes only the audience, place and language wording. At the end, the steps walk you through Google Flow and CapCut.

Updated 27 September 2026

Before you start

0Choose the market and paste the master prompt

Open ChatGPT, start a new chat and turn on Thinking mode. This is your main chat. Copy the master prompt and send it. ChatGPT will answer with one question. Come back here for the reply.

You'll use 4 chats. The main chat plans the ad. The character, product and location sheets are each made in their own side chat, so ChatGPT doesn't lose details. The main chat gives you a brief to paste into each side chat; you bring the approved sheet and a short Handoff Card back.
What you need: a paid ChatGPT plan (so it can make images) and access to Google Flow (part of Google's paid AI plans). CapCut or any video editor for the last step.

Stages 7–10 · Script and storyboard

  
8Approve the script and storyboard

  
Back in the main chat, ChatGPT writes the script (what the person says), plans every shot, draws the storyboards one at a time (up to 6 shots each) and checks everything. It stops and asks after each part. Read each one; if something is wrong, type what to change and copy that reply instead.

  

  
Download every storyboard image. Each panel is a clean picture with one line under it: the shot number and the words, for example 3 · SAYS: "okay so…". No text should be drawn on the pictures themselves.
